# Third-party components and asset policy

## Runtime libraries

- **Three.js** is loaded in the browser from jsDelivr.
- **FastAPI**, **Uvicorn**, **websockets**, **httpx** and **pytest** are installed from `requirements.txt`.
- **Google Fonts** is used for the optional menu typography.

Review and preserve the respective open-source/library license requirements when redistributing the project.

## Game art/assets

The shipped game does **not** contain copied Krunker, Minecraft, EA Sports FC/FIFA, Fortnite or Clash of Clans models, maps, textures, audio, logos or source files. The five environments are procedural/original interpretations of broad gameplay/visual concepts.

This distinction is especially important if the site is monetized or promoted publicly.
